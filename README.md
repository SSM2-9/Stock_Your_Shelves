# Stock Your Shelves

## Deployed using Vercel: https://stock-your-shelves-wdtx.vercel.app

Whether you're replenishing your essentials or adding something new, this is your go-to spot for managing your pantry inventory. Keep your shelves full and your cooking adventures deliciously prepared!
